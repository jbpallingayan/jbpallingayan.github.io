OnePropertee: Platform Test Automation

Role: Automation Test Engineer

Project: A real estate platform connecting buyers, sellers, and property partners through listings, lead management, inquiries, and property visits.

My contribution: Develop and maintain Cypress end-to-end and regression tests across desktop and mobile-web workflows, including authentication, property listings, CRM, buyer and seller interactions, property visits, and inventory.

Test infrastructure: Build reusable test utilities, fixtures, and role-specific setup. Maintain test data and feature-toggle configurations to exercise relevant application states.

CI and troubleshooting: Investigate local and GitLab CI failures using logs, screenshots, and focused reproduction runs. Maintain tests as application behavior changes and collaborate with developers and DevOps on test reliability and coverage reporting.

AI-assisted workflow orchestration: Direct Codex-supported Cypress maintenance and issue investigation by defining the problem, supplying workflow and permission requirements, and setting code-change boundaries. Guide investigations as evidence emerges, review proposed changes, and assess focused test results before accepting a solution.

Contribution: Provide repeatable regression checks for core business workflows and actionable failure evidence to support release decisions.

Tools: Cypress, JavaScript, Git, GitLab CI/CD, Meteor, Node.js, Docker, ChatGPT, Codex
